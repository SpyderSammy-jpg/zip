How to Play

Find the continuous route through the grid. Every square matters.

  1. Start at checkpoint 1.
  2. Move one square at a time, up, down, left, or right.
  3. Visit checkpoints 1 through 15 in order.
  4. Fill all 100 squares without revisiting any square.
  5. Use the arrow keys or click a neighboring square. Blocked edges cannot be crossed.
  6. Undo removes your last move. Restart clears the path and adds 2 to your undo count.
  7. Restart does not reset the timer. The timer stops when you finish.
  8. Only today's daily puzzle updates your streak and total. Calendar puzzles are practice.
  9. Earn up to 3 stars for a fast, efficient solve.
